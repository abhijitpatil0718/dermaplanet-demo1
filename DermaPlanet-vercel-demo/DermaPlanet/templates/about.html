About DermaPlanet

A modern dermatology clinic for skin, hair and cosmetic care.

DermaPlanet brings together experienced dermatologists and up-to-date technology so that you get a clear diagnosis and a plan you understand.

We keep consultations unhurried, explain the options and the likely results, and follow up so treatment can be adjusted when it needs to be.

What you can expect

  • Treatment plans built around your skin or hair, not a standard package
  • Online booking with real, live availability
  • Appointment status and history in your own account
